आम्ही कोणती माहिती गोळा करतो?

काहीही नाही.

  • कोणतेही नाव, ईमेल, फोन नंबर किंवा पत्ता गोळा केला जात नाही
  • कोणताही लॉगिन किंवा अकाऊंट आवश्यक नाही
  • आपली वापराची माहिती (usage data) साठवली जात नाही
  • कोणतेही cookies तयार केले जात नाहीत
  • आपले location, camera, microphone किंवा contacts वापरले जात नाहीत

तृतीय पक्षाच्या सेवा

हे अ‍ॅप GitHub Pages (github.io) वर होस्ट केलेली वेबसाइट लोड करते. त्यामुळे GitHub च्या सर्व्हरशी नेटवर्क कनेक्शन होते. GitHub ची स्वतःची Privacy Policy लागू होते.

अ‍ॅप Chrome किंवा अन्य ब्राऊझरच्या माध्यमातून चालते. Google Chrome ची Privacy Policy वेगळी आहे.

About the App

Gita-Dnyaneshwari is a Marathi spiritual app that presents concepts from the Bhagavad Gita and Sant Dnyaneshwar's Dnyaneshwari in connection with modern life. The app opens a website hosted on GitHub Pages inside an Android Trusted Web Activity (TWA) wrapper.

Information We Collect

None.

  • No name, email, phone number, or address is collected
  • No account or login is required
  • No usage data or analytics are stored
  • No cookies are created by us
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, or contacts

Third-Party Services

The app loads a website hosted on GitHub Pages (github.io), which involves a network connection to GitHub's servers. GitHub's own Privacy Policy applies to that connection.

The app runs via Chrome or another browser engine on your device. Google Chrome's Privacy Policy applies separately.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no data collection of any kind occurs within the app, it is safe for all users.

Internet Permission

The app requires internet permission solely to load the website content. This permission is not used to transmit any user data.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated accordingly. Significant changes will be noted in the app update release notes.
